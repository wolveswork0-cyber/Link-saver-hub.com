Frequently Asked Questions

Some helpful information about LinkDrop Pro.

1. What is LinkDrop Pro?

LinkDrop Pro is an online tool that processes publicly accessible media links from supported platforms.

2. How do I use LinkDrop Pro?

Copy a public media URL, paste it into the input box and press Download. Follow the available options.

3. Which platforms are supported?

Supported platforms may include YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, X and Snapchat. Availability depends on the backend extractor and the individual URL.

4. Is LinkDrop Pro free?

You can access the LinkDrop Pro website without creating an account. Availability of individual services may vary.

5. Do I need an account or password?

No social media password is requested by LinkDrop Pro. Private or login-required content may not be supported.

6. Why is my link not working?

The link may be private, restricted, unsupported, unavailable or temporarily inaccessible. Try a publicly accessible link from a supported platform.

7. Can I use LinkDrop Pro on my phone?

Yes. The website is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets and desktop devices.

8. Can I download private or protected content?

No. Private, login-required, DRM-protected or otherwise restricted content may not be downloadable. Only download content you own or have permission to use.

Important Usage Notice

LinkDrop Pro is intended for publicly accessible media URLs supported by its backend. Private, login-required, DRM-protected or restricted content may not be available. Users are responsible for respecting copyright laws and the terms of the relevant platforms.
